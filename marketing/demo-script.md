# QuoteFlow — 90-second demo video script

**Goal:** one forwarder watches this (probably muted, on a phone) and thinks
"that would save me hours." It is the asset you drop into a DM/WhatsApp and
embed on the landing page.

**The golden rule:** assume **no sound**. Burn in on-screen captions that tell
the whole story by themselves. The voiceover is a bonus, not the carrier.

---

## Before you record — prep checklist

- [ ] App running, logged in as the demo owner.
- [ ] **Settings → logo set** to a real-looking forwarder logo (the "DEMO
      LOGISTICS" one is fine, or your own).
- [ ] One **clean customer** exists with a believable name (e.g. "Sunrise
      Trading Co"), not "Validated Co". Delete clutter/test customers.
- [ ] Delete old test rate sets / quotes so the lists look tidy.
- [ ] `ratesheet_test.pdf` (the Meridian sheet) saved somewhere easy to pick.
- [ ] Browser: full screen, **hide the bookmarks bar**, clean profile, zoom
      110–125% so text survives shrinking to a phone.
- [ ] Cursor click-highlight ON (Loom does this automatically).

## Specs

- **Record 1920×1080 (16:9).** Also export a **1:1 square** crop for WhatsApp/
  feed — keep the key action centred so it survives the crop.
- **Length: 75–90s. Cut ruthlessly.** Speed-ramp (2–4×) any loading/typing.
- Tool: **Loom** (instant share link, auto cursor highlight) or **OBS** (more
  control). Either is fine.
- Music: subtle, upbeat, low under the VO (Pixabay / YouTube Audio Library).
- **Burn in captions.** Sans-serif, bottom third, high contrast.

## Clean voiceover block (read this straight through ≈ 95 words / ~80s)

> Every quote starts the same way — a carrier's rate sheet, and twenty minutes
> of re-typing. Or two.
> Upload the sheet — PDF, Excel, even a scan. QuoteFlow reads every lane,
> container and charge, and flags anything it's unsure of, so a wrong rate never
> reaches your customer.
> Pick your customer, set your markup — and your quote is priced instantly, USD
> and ZAR kept exactly right. A clean, branded quote, with your logo, not ours.
> Accepted? Turn it into an invoice in one click.
> Stop re-typing rate sheets. Send us your messiest one — we'll quote it back,
> free.

**Tone:** calm, confident, plain. Your own voice (a local accent *builds* trust
with SADC forwarders — don't use a generic American TTS if you can avoid it).

## Captions-only version (if you record silent)

Same beats, just the **bold captions** above, each held ~3–4s. The video must
make complete sense with zero audio — test it muted before you publish.

## After you've recorded

1. Drop the file into the landing page (`DEMO_VIDEO` placeholder → embed).
2. Post it natively on LinkedIn (don't just link YouTube — native autoplays).
3. Attach the square version to WhatsApp outreach.
4. The first real customer's reaction becomes your next, better video.
