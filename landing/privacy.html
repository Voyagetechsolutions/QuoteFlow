This policy explains how [Your registered company name] ("we") handles personal information when you use QuoteFlow, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. What we collect

  • Account & company details — name, email, password (hashed), company name, logo, VAT number.
  • Your business content — rate sheets you upload, and the customers, quotes and invoices you create.
  • Technical data — basic logs needed to run and secure the Service.

2. How we use it

Only to provide and improve the Service: authenticating you, extracting rates, generating your quotes and invoices, and keeping the Service secure. We do not sell your data.

3. Who we share it with (operators)

We use trusted processors who act on our instructions:

  • Hosting & database — our cloud hosting and Neon (managed PostgreSQL) store your data.
  • AI extraction — when a rate sheet needs it, the page image is sent to a vision model via OpenRouter to read it. Don't upload sheets you're not permitted to process.
  • Payments — if you subscribe, payment details are handled by our payment provider (e.g. Stripe); we don't store card numbers.

Some of these process data outside South Africa; we rely on their safeguards for lawful cross-border transfer under POPIA.

4. Security

Passwords are hashed (bcrypt), traffic is encrypted in transit, access is scoped per company, and the API is rate-limited and hardened. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

5. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, then delete or anonymise it unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your rights under POPIA

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
